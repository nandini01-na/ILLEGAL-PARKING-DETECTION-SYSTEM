INSPECTION DEMO — 60 to 90 seconds

1. “Our system takes CCTV/webcam or recorded video as input.”
2. Start Webcam or upload a parking/road video.
3. “YOLO detects vehicles in real time.”
4. Point out the red rectangle: “This is the predefined restricted/no-parking zone.”
5. When a vehicle enters and stays in the zone, explain: “The system tracks the vehicle and after the configured persistence time it flags a potential illegal parking violation.”
6. Show the red ILLEGAL PARKING label.
7. Show Recent Violation Records: “The incident is automatically recorded with vehicle type, time and status.”
8. “Our current prototype focuses on detection, zone analysis, alerting and basic records. Automatic fines and government database integration are outside the initial scope.”
